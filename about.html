About Griggs Street Lighting

A trusted name in Epping's electrical and street lighting industry for over 25 years.

Our Story

Built on Trust, Expertise & Hard Work

Griggs Street Lighting & Maintenance has been serving clients across Epping and Essex for over 25 years. What began as a passion for the electrical trade has grown into one of the most trusted street lighting and electrical contractors in the region.

We've built our business the right way — through quality workmanship, honest pricing and a genuine commitment to our customers' needs. Whether we're installing a single lamp post or undertaking a large-scale public lighting project, every job receives the same level of care and attention.

Our team consists of qualified, experienced engineers who take real pride in the work they do. We believe that when a job is done right, it speaks for itself — and our long-standing reputation in Epping and beyond is proof of that.
