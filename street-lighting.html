Our Expertise

Reliable Street Lighting Service

At Griggs Street Lighting & Maintenance, we offer a comprehensive street lighting installation service. Whether you need street lights installed or public lighting work, you can rely on us. We have the equipment and experience to take on any type of street lighting project.

This includes digging the trench to installing the lamps and connecting them to the electrics. We also carry out external property lighting projects, from commercial buildings such as hotels and schools, to lighting for homes and gardens.

You can rest assured of getting thorough and reliable services at very reasonable prices from our highly qualified team.
